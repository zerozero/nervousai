Movement that fits the day you actually have

The Movement is an AI coach for people who sit too much and want to stay strong for the decades ahead. Two-to-five-minute exercise snacks, guided by voice, matched to how you feel today. No gym. No shame. No "lose 10 kg in 4 weeks".

Now recruiting 10 alpha testers (iPhone, Nov–Dec 2026). Free.

Three quick questions and your email: see Join the alpha below.

Who it's for

Desk workers and comeback athletes, roughly 40 to 60. You have started thinking about ageing, perhaps after watching a parent or a friend decline. You know you should move more. Fitness culture puts you off, and the day never has an hour in it.

How it works

  1. Check in by voice. Thirty seconds on how you slept, how you feel, what hurts.
  2. Get a snack. Two to five minutes of movement, chosen for your energy, your joints and wherever you are: desk, kitchen, hotel room.
  3. Keep the when, adapt the how. A bad night means a gentler snack, not a skipped one. Rest is part of the plan.

What makes it different

  • Compassion over competition. The coach never shames, compares or nags.
  • Snacks over sessions. Short bursts through the day do more for healthspan than one heroic workout you never get to.
  • Adapt the HOW, maintain the WHEN. The habit survives because the ask flexes with you.
  • Weight is not a metric. We measure consistency, energy and how you feel, nothing else.
